NaviRing — Data Safety

18/09/2026 · Ứng dụng: NaviRing (com.gioitv.naviring) · Liên hệ: gioitv86@gmail.com

1. Summary

QuestionAnswer
Does the app collect user data?No. All data is processed on the phone and never sent to the developer
Does the app share data with third parties?No
Does the app have Internet access?No — it cannot send data anywhere
Any advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs?No
Is data encrypted in transit?Not applicable — the app sends nothing over a network
Can users delete their data?Yes — in the app or by uninstalling

Your contacts are never read or changed. The app only creates, reads and deletes its own entries in a separate "NaviRing" account that is not synced to the cloud.

3. Ads

The app introduces NaviCast, another app by the same developer. The promotion is built into the app, uses no ad network, and collects or sends no data. Tapping it only opens NaviCast on Google Play.

4. Deleting your data

  • Delete one or all names under Saved caller names.
  • Clear the log under Technical log.
  • Uninstall the app: Android removes all data and the "NaviRing" account.

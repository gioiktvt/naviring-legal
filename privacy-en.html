NaviRing — Privacy Policy

18/09/2026 · Ứng dụng: NaviRing (com.gioitv.naviring) · Liên hệ: gioitv86@gmail.com

1. Summary

NaviRing makes calls from OTT apps ring on your Bluetooth watch. The app has no Internet permission, no account, and no third-party analytics or ad SDKs. Data is processed on your phone and is not sent to us or anyone else.

2. Data the app processes

DataPurposeWhere and how long
Incoming-call notifications from OTT apps you enable (caller name, call state, answer/decline buttons)Detect calls, show the name on the watch, forward answer/decline from the watchMemory only, during the call. Other notifications and message content are ignored immediately, never read or stored
Caller name and app name (e.g. "Zalo · Minh Anh")Let the watch look up the name over BluetoothStored in a separate on-device account named "NaviRing" (Contacts) with a placeholder number starting with 0000. Never synced to the cloud
Your contactsNot used. The app only creates, reads and deletes entries it created itself—
Watch Bluetooth address and nameLink the watch so the app starts when it connectsOn device until uninstall
Technical log (event codes, times, OTT app package, error codes)TroubleshootingOn device, up to 300 lines; no names, numbers or content. Leaves the phone only if you tap "Share"
Settings (enabled OTT apps, on-duty state)Remember your choicesOn device

3. Data leaving the phone

  • To your own watch over Bluetooth, by the Android system: the call's name and placeholder number. The watch maker's app may keep it under its own policy.
  • Nothing is sent to our servers (the app has no Internet permission).
  • When you tap the NaviCast promotion, Google Play opens with a source parameter (utm_source=naviring) for install statistics; no personal data is included.

4. Android permissions

PermissionReason
Notification accessDetect incoming-call notifications from OTT apps
Contacts (read/write)Save caller names so the watch shows them; without it the watch shows a number
Manage own calls (MANAGE_OWN_CALLS)Create a call on the phone so the watch rings; no cellular call, no charges
Companion device (watch)Start automatically when the watch connects
Manage ongoing calls (granted with the watch role)Detect when an OTT app already sends its own call to the watch, to avoid a duplicate. Names and numbers are not read and that call is not controlled

5. Your choices

Turn off "Ring my watch for app calls", disable individual OTT apps, delete saved names, revoke permissions in Android Settings, or uninstall the app (Android removes the NaviRing account and saved names).

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly process children's data.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date at the top will be updated.

NaviRing is an independent app, not affiliated with any OTT app developer or watch maker.
